One thing to pin down

"Close thread" and "Delete thread" — should Close be reversible (archives the thread and all its tasks, keeps full history for AI to reference later, hidden from the main canvas but reopenable) while Delete is permanent removal? That's the standard split and matters for the later AI catch-up feature, which needs history to still exist for closed threads.
